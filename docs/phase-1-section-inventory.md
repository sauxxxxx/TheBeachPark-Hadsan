# Phase 1 Homepage Section Inventory

Source of truth: proposal PDF pages 2-5. The PDF is a 1440 x 810 landscape presentation; the homepage is shown as four continuous desktop captures.

## Global visual system

- Warm ivory canvas with deep navy text and coral-orange calls to action.
- Large high-contrast editorial serif headlines; compact, bold uppercase sans-serif labels and navigation.
- Aqua hand-drawn wave marks appear as restrained dividers.
- Photography drives the hierarchy: full-bleed hero, tightly cropped food and activity features, and overlapping white-bordered gallery images.
- Most sections use square corners. Image overlap, scale contrast, and asymmetric columns create the spatial character.
- The hero and content sections use wide, desktop-first compositions with deliberate negative space.
- Thin coral rules divide sections and footer metadata.
- The footer is a solid deep navy block with a large logo, five navigation columns, contact details, social links, and two CTAs.

## Exact homepage sequence

### 1. Header and aerial hero

- Full-bleed aerial beachfront resort photograph, approximately 16:7 on desktop.
- Large white logo panel overlaps the upper-left of the hero.
- Navigation floats over the photograph across the top center; coral filled booking button at upper-right.
- Headline begins around 22% from the left and 24% from the top: three-line navy serif, roughly 92-108px at a 1440px viewport.
- Coral handwritten phrase sits below the headline, followed by a short navy paragraph and two side-by-side CTAs.
- Mobile inference: logo and compact menu occupy an ivory bar; headline and CTAs remain over a darker image scrim; hero becomes portrait-oriented.

### 2. Five ways to make waves

- Ivory background and left-aligned two-line serif heading.
- Asymmetric editorial collage to the right: wide shoreline/resort image, café/deck image, then overlapping Sharky's, Waterdog, and souvenir images with white borders.
- Small uppercase labels appear inside image corners.
- Mobile inference: heading first, followed by one large image and a two-column grid; overlaps are reduced but retained.

### 3. Make a day of it timeline

- Full-width sunset shoreline photograph with a light ivory wash for text legibility.
- Coral script headline at left.
- Six horizontal timeline stops: 8 AM coffee, 10 AM beach and pool, 12 PM lunch, 2 PM Waterdog, 5:30 PM sunset, 6 PM souvenir shop.
- Thin coral vertical separators.
- Mobile inference: image remains full width; timeline becomes a two-column or vertically scrolling list.

### 4. Stay for more than the night

- Ivory background with a dominant wide room image on the left.
- Editorial heading and short copy centered in the middle-right with aqua wave accent and coral CTA.
- Two smaller room crops stacked at the far right.
- Mobile inference: heading and copy precede one large room image; secondary room images become a two-column strip.

### 5. Dining features

- Two editorial blocks share a single row.
- Beans & Paddles: copy on ivory at left, large latte close-up beside it.
- Sharky's Café: large seafood pasta image followed by copy on ivory at right.
- Both blocks use uppercase venue labels, serif headings, aqua wave marks, and concise supporting copy.
- Mobile inference: each venue becomes its own alternating text/image block.

### 6. Waterdog Adventures

- Full-width turquoise action image with a jet ski moving through the right two-thirds.
- Left text block is integrated over calm water: uppercase label, condensed uppercase headline, short description, coral CTA.
- Mobile inference: taller crop with darkened left/bottom text-safe area.

### 7. Made to be remembered

- Ivory collage section with serif heading and short copy at left.
- Overlapping, lightly rotated, white-bordered photographs: sunset paddle, pool group, palms/shore, food, banana boat, sunset palms, and souvenir shopping.
- Images touch and overlap the navy footer to create a continuous magazine spread.
- Mobile inference: masonry-like two-column collage with smaller rotation and controlled overlap.

### 8. Footer

- Deep navy background.
- Large Beach Park logo at left.
- Columns: Stay, Eat, Adventures, Explore, Offers, Contact.
- Coral filled "Book Your Stay" and coral-outlined "Plan Your Beach Day" buttons at right.
- Copyright, privacy, and terms sit along a thin upper/lower metadata rail.
